How it works

  1. Free analysis first. Book a free Credit Review & Repair Consultation, submit the free analysis request, or call (929) 244-0744. We look at your situation and goals and produce a written analysis: what appears inaccurate, incomplete or unverifiable, what is accurate and cannot be disputed, and the rebuild play for the rest. The analysis is free, and it is not a contract.
  2. Your rights, in writing, signed separately. Before any enrollment you receive the federal Consumer Credit File Rights statement as its own document, then a written agreement stating the services and the total of all payments, with the cancellation notice and two copies of the Notice of Cancellation form.
  3. Three business days. No work is performed and no payment is taken until your three-business-day cancellation period has passed and you have not cancelled.
  4. Work first, then billing. Your first charge happens only after the work it pays for has been fully performed and documented. After that, plans bill monthly in arrears for the month of work just completed — and billing pauses automatically in any month where no work was delivered.
  5. Rounds, reviews, rebuild. Dispute rounds run on a roughly 45-day cycle; you re-pull reports through your monitoring so each round is verified, and your plan sequences the rebuilding steps around the disputes.

Our red-line commitments

  • We never guarantee a credit score, a deletion, or a timeline — the claim itself is illegal, whoever makes it.
  • We never charge in advance. Work is performed and documented first; billing follows.
  • We never ask for your Social Security number on a web form, and we never hold your credit monitoring password — ever.
  • We never dispute information you confirm is accurate, and we never file a false identity-theft claim.
  • We do not sell, rent or broker tradelines, and we do not offer "new credit identities" or CPNs — that is fraud, and our cheapest guide exists to warn you off it.
  • We never sell your information. Your reports and documents are yours.

The honest fine print — up front

You can dispute credit report errors yourself, for free, without paying any company — the federal AnnualCreditReport.com site and the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau explain how. No credit repair company, including Fiscally Fit, can remove accurate, current, verifiable information from a credit report, and no one can legally promise you a specific credit score, a deletion, or a date by which your credit will change. We sell the work, the organisation and the follow-through of disputing information you believe is inaccurate, incomplete or unverifiable — never an outcome. Fiscally Fit is a credit services organisation, not a law firm, tax advisor, mortgage lender or debt settlement company, and nothing on this site is legal, tax or investment advice.
