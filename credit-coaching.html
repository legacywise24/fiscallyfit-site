When the file is accurate, the answer is a rebuild

A large share of the people who ask about credit repair do not have a dispute problem — they have accurate late payments, high utilisation and thin positive history. Disputing accurate information is not a strategy. Coaching is the honest product for that file: a structured rebuild of the habits and account architecture that scoring models actually read.

The 90-Day Rebuild Sprint

  • Utilisation engineering — per-card and aggregate balances, statement-date versus due-date mechanics (the full method is Guide #2, Utilisation & Scoring Mechanics)
  • Autopay architecture so a payment can never be late by accident again
  • Builder-product sequencing — at most two builder products at a time, matched to your file (see Rent Reporting & Credit Builders)
  • A written 90-day calendar: what to pay, what to open, what to leave alone, and when to re-pull

The sprint is a fixed-scope engagement, billed in monthly instalments in arrears — you are never asked to pay for coaching that has not happened yet.

The honest fine print — up front

You can dispute credit report errors yourself, for free, without paying any company — the federal AnnualCreditReport.com site and the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau explain how. No credit repair company, including Fiscally Fit, can remove accurate, current, verifiable information from a credit report, and no one can legally promise you a specific credit score, a deletion, or a date by which your credit will change. We sell the work, the organisation and the follow-through of disputing information you believe is inaccurate, incomplete or unverifiable — never an outcome. Fiscally Fit is a credit services organisation, not a law firm, tax advisor, mortgage lender or debt settlement company, and nothing on this site is legal, tax or investment advice.
