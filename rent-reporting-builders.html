Rent reporting — your largest payment, finally on the record

Rent is the biggest monthly payment most people make, and historically it reported nowhere. Rent-reporting services change that by furnishing your on-time rent to the credit bureaus. It is an optional add-on you buy from the provider — never bundled into our fee, never a condition of our service — and it belongs in a Tier 2/3 plan or a coaching sprint where it is sequenced with everything else.

Esusu

Reports eligible on-time rent to all three bureaus and runs formal partner programmes, including a referral track renters can join at a discount. See esusurent.com.

RentReporters

Rent reporting with a property-manager channel — often the cleanest route is your landlord offering it as a tenant benefit. See rentreporters.com.

Credit-builder products — at most two at a time

Builder products work, and they are also the easiest upsell in this industry. Our rule: no client is put on more than two builder products at once — stacking five small tradelines is a churn pattern and a drain on your cash, not a strategy.

The pledge / share-secured loan

The most under-sold legitimate builder there is: borrow against your own deposit at a credit union, the payments report to the bureaus, and the deposit unlocks as you pay. No affiliate pays us for recommending it. We recommend it anyway.

Kikoff

A low-cost reported tradeline (plans from $5/month, no credit check) suited to no-file and thin-file clients, billed by Kikoff. See kikoff.com.

Self — Credit Builder Account

A bank-partner certificate-of-deposit structure that reports to all three bureaus while you build savings. See self.inc.

Secured cards

Issuer secured cards and secured Visa-class products, chosen per file and always paired with the utilisation rule from Guide #2: small balances, statement-date awareness, autopay in full.

Authorised users — family only

A family member's seasoned card can help; a stranger's card, rented for a fee, is the tradeline market — read Guide #6 before anyone sells you one. We never sell, rent or broker tradelines.

The honest fine print — up front

You can dispute credit report errors yourself, for free, without paying any company — the federal AnnualCreditReport.com site and the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau explain how. No credit repair company, including Fiscally Fit, can remove accurate, current, verifiable information from a credit report, and no one can legally promise you a specific credit score, a deletion, or a date by which your credit will change. We sell the work, the organisation and the follow-through of disputing information you believe is inaccurate, incomplete or unverifiable — never an outcome. Fiscally Fit is a credit services organisation, not a law firm, tax advisor, mortgage lender or debt settlement company, and nothing on this site is legal, tax or investment advice.
