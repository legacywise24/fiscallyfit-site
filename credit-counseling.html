Counseling is the main engine — here is why

Most people who call a credit repair company do not first need disputes filed. They need someone qualified to sit with the whole picture — three credit reports, the balances, the income, the goal — and turn it into a plan they understand and can run. That is credit counseling, and it is the front door of this firm: every engagement here starts with a counseling conversation, and for many clients counseling is the complete service — no repair contract, no dispute rounds, nothing sold past the need.

What ongoing counseling covers

  • Budget & cash-flow build — a working view of income, recurring expenses and debt obligations, and a payment calendar you can actually keep
  • Credit report literacy — reading all three reports line by line: what each entry is, what it does, how long it lawfully stays, and what can and cannot change
  • Utilisation & payment architecture — the mechanics of Guide #2 applied to your balances, with autopay set up so a late payment becomes structurally difficult
  • Goal plans — homebuyer readiness, auto financing readiness and rental readiness timelines, with the lender's decision always named as the lender's — we prepare you; we never promise an approval
  • Hardship triage — job loss, medical events, divorce, military service: what to say to creditors, in what order, and when the right answer is an attorney or a nonprofit agency instead of us
  • Identity-theft education — the free federal recovery path at IdentityTheft.gov, walked through with you before any paid conversation (see Your Rights)

What counseling is not

We are not a debt settlement company and we do not negotiate your balances down for a fee, take possession of your funds, or run a debt management plan that pays your creditors for you — those are different, separately regulated businesses, and blurring them is how consumers get hurt. We do not provide legal, tax or investment advice. And counseling aimed at improving a credit rating is treated here as covered by the same federal rules as repair: same written disclosures, same cancellation rights, same no-advance-fee discipline — we do not use the word "counseling" to step around the law.

Counseling or repair — which do you need?

Repair addresses information on your reports that you believe is inaccurate, incomplete or unverifiable. Counseling addresses everything else: accurate history, balances, habits, timing and goals. Most real files need some of both, sequenced — counseling first to stabilise the picture, repair rounds where the record itself is wrong. The free Credit Review exists to make that call honestly, in writing, before you sign anything.

The honest fine print — up front

You can dispute credit report errors yourself, for free, without paying any company — the federal AnnualCreditReport.com site and the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau explain how. No credit repair company, including Fiscally Fit, can remove accurate, current, verifiable information from a credit report, and no one can legally promise you a specific credit score, a deletion, or a date by which your credit will change. We sell the work, the organisation and the follow-through of disputing information you believe is inaccurate, incomplete or unverifiable — never an outcome. Fiscally Fit is a credit services organisation, not a law firm, tax advisor, mortgage lender or debt settlement company, and nothing on this site is legal, tax or investment advice.
