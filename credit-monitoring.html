Why monitoring is part of the work

A dispute round is only finished when a fresh report proves what changed. That takes current three-bureau data, pulled on the round cycle — which is why every full-service plan works alongside a monitoring subscription. Free reports at AnnualCreditReport.com are a genuine alternative for a one-time look; they do not provide ongoing monitoring or scores.

Monitoring is the provider's product — not ours

Read this before you click anything: credit monitoring is sold and billed by the monitoring company, under your agreement with them. Cancelling Fiscally Fit does not cancel your monitoring, and cancelling monitoring does not cancel Fiscally Fit. We never see, ask for, or store your monitoring username or password — report access happens through secure import tools or by you uploading your own reports. And a disclosure we make in writing, here and in every agreement: we may receive compensation if you enroll through our links, at no additional cost to you.

The honest fine print — up front

You can dispute credit report errors yourself, for free, without paying any company — the federal AnnualCreditReport.com site and the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau explain how. No credit repair company, including Fiscally Fit, can remove accurate, current, verifiable information from a credit report, and no one can legally promise you a specific credit score, a deletion, or a date by which your credit will change. We sell the work, the organisation and the follow-through of disputing information you believe is inaccurate, incomplete or unverifiable — never an outcome. Fiscally Fit is a credit services organisation, not a law firm, tax advisor, mortgage lender or debt settlement company, and nothing on this site is legal, tax or investment advice.
